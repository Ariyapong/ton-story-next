A back-office console for an internal team. Replaces three older spreadsheet
workflows with a single keyboard-driven UI.

## What I built

- Role-based access for ~40 internal users
- Tabular forms with inline edit + bulk actions
- Optimistic updates over a slow corporate API

## What I learned

Ant Design is fine when you stop fighting it. The trick was theming it once,
deeply, instead of overriding individual components per page.
