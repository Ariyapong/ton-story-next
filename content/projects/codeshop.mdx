A focused two-screen application: cashier issues a code, customer redeems it.
Most of the work was making the failure modes obvious — disconnected
register, duplicate scan, expired code.

## Constraint

Had to run reliably on tablets older than the project itself. Smaller bundle,
fewer dependencies, no animation that wasn't load-bearing.
